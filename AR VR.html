A-to-Z Learning Modules

Level 1: 3D Mathematics, Unity C# & Basic VR Rigs

Coordinate systems, Euler vs Quaternions, physics Raycasting, and deploying your first interactive VR environment.

1. Spatial Math & Vectors
Cartesian 3D coordinates, vector dot & cross products, normalized vectors, and Quaternion rotations.
2. Unity Engine & C# Architecture
GameObjects, components, MonoBehaviours, Update loop lifecycle, Raycasts, and rigid body physics.
3. OpenXR & Headset Camera Setup
Configuring the OpenXR plugin, setting up 6-DoF tracking cameras, and controller pose mapping.
4. XR Interaction Toolkit (XRI)
Grab interactables, continuous movement vs teleportation locomotion, snap turning, and UI ray interactors.

Level 2: AR Foundation, Hand Tracking, Shaders & Spatial Audio

Surface plane detection, controller-free hand tracking, Universal Render Pipeline (URP), and directional audio.

1. AR Foundation & Mobile AR
ARKit/ARCore plane detection, point clouds, light estimation, raycasting onto real surfaces, and image tracking.
2. Hand Tracking & Pinch Gestures
Skeletal hand models, joint transforms, pinch detection, poke interactors, and palm-menu interfaces.
3. URP Shaders & Visual Effects
Shader Graph creation, transparent holographic materials, fresnel effects, and particle system simulation.
4. Binaural Spatial Audio
HRTF (Head-Related Transfer Function) spatial audio emitters, sound occlusion, and room acoustics reverbs.

Level 3: visionOS, Unreal C++, Gaussian Splats & GPU Optimization

Apple RealityKit, eye-gaze targeting, high-performance rendering optimization, and neural 3D scene captures.

1. Apple visionOS & RealityKit
SwiftUI spatial windows, volumes, immersive spaces, Reality Composer Pro, and eye-tracking targeting.
2. Unreal Engine 5 for High-Fidelity XR
C++ architecture, Nanite virtualized geometry, Lumen lighting in VR, and high-end training simulators.
3. 3D Gaussian Splatting & WebXR
Rendering real-world photogrammetry captures at 90+ FPS and building lightweight Three.js WebXR applications.
4. Deep Performance Profiling
RenderDoc debugging, Unity Profiler, eliminating garbage collection spikes, dynamic foveated rendering, and GPU instancing.
