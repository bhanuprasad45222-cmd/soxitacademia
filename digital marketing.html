A-to-Z Learning Modules

Level 1: SEO, Content & Digital Foundations

Organic search strategies, social media management, content creation, and basic traffic analysis.

1. Search Engine Optimization (SEO)
Keyword research, on-page optimization, meta tags, and understanding search intent.
2. Content Marketing
Writing high-converting blog posts, creating content calendars, and storytelling frameworks.
3. Social Media Management
Organic strategy across LinkedIn, X, Instagram, TikTok, and community engagement.
4. Basic Web Analytics
Navigating Google Analytics 4 (GA4), tracking page views, bounce rates, and basic audience behavior.

Level 2: Performance Marketing, CRO & Automation

Running profitable ad campaigns, configuring event tracking, and building automated email workflows.

1. Paid Acquisition (PPC & Social)
Managing Google Search/Display and Meta Ads, audience targeting, and bidding strategies.
2. Advanced Analytics & Tagging
Configuring custom events and conversion tracking using Google Tag Manager (GTM).
3. CRM & Email Marketing
Building automated lead-nurturing workflows and segmentation using HubSpot or Klaviyo.
4. Conversion Rate Optimization (CRO)
Designing high-converting landing pages and running A/B tests using tools like Optimizely or VWO.

Level 3: Growth Hacking, Attribution & AI Strategy

Full-funnel growth loops, multi-touch attribution modeling, and scaling with AI.

1. Growth Hacking & Viral Loops
Designing referral programs, optimizing the Pirate Funnel (AARRR), and rapid experimentation.
2. Multi-Touch Attribution Modeling
Mapping complex user journeys across multiple channels to assign accurate ROI to campaigns.
3. Predictive Analytics & LTV
Using data to predict customer churn, calculate true LTV, and optimize allowable CAC.
4. AI-Driven Marketing Automation
Scaling content generation, programmatic SEO, and omnichannel personalization using AI tools.
