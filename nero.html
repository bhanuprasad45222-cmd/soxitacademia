A-to-Z Learning Modules

Level 1: Neurobiology Foundations, Spiking Math & SNN Basics

Biological neural signaling, differential membrane equations, rate vs temporal coding, and PyTorch SNN toolkits.

1. Computational Neuroscience Basics
Action potentials, synaptic transmission, Hodgkin-Huxley theory, and ionic equilibrium potentials.
2. Leaky Integrate-and-Fire (LIF) Math
Membrane resistance and capacitance, linear differential equations, reset thresholds, and refractory states.
3. Spike Encoding Schemes
Translating analog signals: rate coding, latency coding, phase coding, and delta modulation encoding.
4. SNN Training with snnTorch
Constructing spiking networks in PyTorch, using surrogate gradient functions, and classifying N-MNIST benchmark data.

Level 2: Event-Based Vision, Intel Lava, Plasticity & FPGAs

Dynamic Vision Sensors (DVS), STDP continuous learning, Intel Lava SDK, and hardware emulation on FPGAs.

1. Dynamic Vision Sensors (DVS Cameras)
Capturing per-pixel brightness change events (x, y, timestamp, polarity) with microsecond latency and zero motion blur.
2. Spike-Timing-Dependent Plasticity (STDP)
Unsupervised bio-plausible learning rules: strengthening or depressing synaptic weights based on spike arrival order.
3. Intel Lava Software Framework
Building asynchronous process graphs, event channels, and preparing algorithms for Intel Loihi 2 execution.
4. FPGA Emulation & AER Bus
Prototyping digital spiking neuron arrays on Xilinx/Intel FPGAs using Address-Event Representation routing.

Level 3: Asynchronous VLSI, Memristive Arrays & Edge Chips

Analog mixed-signal design, in-memory computing with memristors, and commercial neuromorphic chip production.

1. Asynchronous Digital Circuit Design
Clockless logic, quasi-delay-insensitive (QDI) circuits, 4-phase handshaking, and low-latency spike routing.
2. In-Memory Computing & Memristors
Resistive RAM (RRAM), phase-change memory (PCM), crossbar matrix-vector multiplication, and analog synapse physics.
3. Chip Architectures (Loihi / Akida / Speck)
Deep-dive into multi-core neuromorphic meshes, on-chip synaptic memory hierarchies, and sub-milliwatt power budgets.
4. Ultra-Fast Edge Robotics & BMIs
Closed-loop high-speed drone stabilization, prosthetic neural signal decoding, and auditory cochlear implants.
