FAULT TOLERANCE
From NISQ to Logical Qubits The breakthrough milestone in quantum computing is transitioning from noisy physical qubits to fault-tolerant logical qubits protected by continuous stabilizer syndrome measurements and real-time error decoding.
Quantum Error Correction (QEC) requires thousands of physical qubits to encode a single fault-tolerant logical qubit, creating huge demand for decoding algorithms (MWPM) and real-time FPGA control logic.

A-to-Z Learning Modules

Level 1: Mathematics of Quantum Mechanics & Quantum Circuit Basics

Vector spaces, tensor products, Dirac bra-ket notation, single and multi-qubit gate logic.

1. Linear Algebra & Hilbert Spaces
Complex vector spaces, inner products, unitary matrices, Hermitian operators, eigenvalues, and tensor products.
2. Qubit Physics & Dirac Notation
Bloch sphere geometric representation, superposition, measurement postulates, and bra-ket formalism (|0⟩ and |1⟩).
3. Quantum Gates & Entanglement
Pauli X, Y, Z, Hadamard, S and T phase gates, CNOT entangling gates, Bell state generation, and quantum teleportation.
4. Quantum SDKs & Statevector Simulation
Setting up IBM Qiskit and Google Cirq, constructing QuantumCircuit instances, and analyzing probability amplitudes.

Level 2: Canonical Algorithms, NISQ Optimization & Hardware Transpilation

Deutsch-Jozsa, Grover's search, variational algorithms, noise models, and cloud QPU execution.

1. Foundational Quantum Algorithms
Deutsch-Jozsa, Bernstein-Vazirani, Simon's algorithm, and Grover's search with oracle amplification.
2. Variational Quantum Eigensolver (VQE)
Parameterizing ansätze circuits, measuring Hamiltonian expectation values, and classical optimization loops (COBYLA/SPSA).
3. QAOA for Combinatorial Optimization
Quantum Approximate Optimization Algorithm for Max-Cut and graph partitioning problems on current NISQ devices.
4. Hardware Execution & Transpilation
Compiling circuits to physical IBM/Rigetti topologies, gate decomposition, readout error mitigation, and zero-noise extrapolation.

Level 3: Fault-Tolerant Quantum Computing, QEC & Quantum Machine Learning

Surface codes, syndrome extraction, Minimum Weight Perfect Matching, PennyLane QML, and Post-Quantum Cryptography.

1. Quantum Error Correction (Surface Codes)
Stabilizer formalism, bit-flip/phase-flip codes, X and Z syndrome extraction, logical qubit encoding, and MWPM decoders.
2. Quantum Phase Estimation & Shor's
Quantum Fourier Transform (QFT), modular exponentiation circuits, Shor's factoring algorithm, and RSA break implications.
3. Quantum Machine Learning (PennyLane)
Parameter-shift rule, quantum neural networks (QNNs), quantum kernel methods, and barren plateau avoidance.
4. Post-Quantum Cryptography (PQC)
NIST-standardized lattice-based encryption (ML-KEM / Kyber, ML-DSA / Dilithium) and enterprise cryptographic transitions.
