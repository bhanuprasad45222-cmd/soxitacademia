A-to-Z Learning Modules

Level 1: Design Thinking, UX Research & Visual Fundamentals

Understanding the user, crafting wireframes, and mastering color, typography, and grids.

1. Design Thinking & Empathy
User interviews, formulating problem statements, and creating user personas/journey maps.
2. Information Architecture (IA)
Card sorting, sitemaps, user flows, and structuring navigation for intuitive discovery.
3. Wireframing & Figma Basics
Low-fidelity sketching, Figma interface basics, frames, auto-layout, and constraints.
4. Visual Design Principles
Color theory, typographic hierarchy, spacing (8pt grid system), contrast, and visual balance.

Level 2: High-Fidelity Prototyping, Systems & Usability Testing

Building scalable components, animating transitions, and validating designs with real users.

1. Design Systems & Tokens
Creating scalable Figma component libraries, variants, and managing color/spacing variables.
2. Advanced Prototyping
Interactive components, smart animate, scrolling behaviors, and realistic app transitions.
3. Usability Testing & Analytics
Conducting moderated/unmoderated testing, analyzing heatmaps, and iterating based on feedback.
4. Developer Handoff & Accessibility
Figma Dev Mode, redlining, ensuring WCAG contrast compliance, and screen-reader logic.

Level 3: Spatial Computing, 3D Integration & Product Strategy

Designing for AR/VR, embedding 3D assets, and leading cross-functional product vision.

1. Spatial UI (VisionOS / Quest)
Designing glass interfaces, managing depth (Z-axis), field of view ergonomics, and gestural inputs.
2. 3D Design & Integration
Creating lightweight 3D scenes in Spline or Blender and embedding WebGL elements into standard UIs.
3. AI in Design Workflows
Using Midjourney/Dall-E for asset generation, and leveraging Figma AI for rapid layout iteration.
4. Product Strategy & Leadership
Aligning design with business OKRs, leading design sprints, and pitching product vision to stakeholders.
